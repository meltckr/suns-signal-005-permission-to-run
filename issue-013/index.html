01 · Ownership

Carry It Through

Jordan Goodwin needed only a few minutes in Detroit to show what an extra possession can look like. He collected three offensive rebounds in his first three minutes. A guard kept giving Phoenix another chance. [Voita · Detroit, Oct. 5]

The ownership question I would keep in view is how much of that good play belongs to a particular group, and how much carries over when the group changes.

In Chicago’s first quarter, Phoenix forced back-to-back turnovers, capped by Ryan Dunn forcing an eight-second violation. Different actions, the same benefit: making the opponent work before Phoenix had to create a shot. [Bowser · Chicago game, Oct. 7]

Phoenix lost 109–107 in Detroit and 124–117 in Chicago after leading by 19 and 15. In Detroit, the starters finished their night at halftime; the players who followed had to sustain the lead against an opponent still using much of its starting group. The later stretches gave Jordan Ott evidence about where that continuity still breaks down. [Voita · Detroit, Oct. 5] [Bowser · Chicago game, Oct. 7]

Saturday’s 102–98 win put that question into the final possessions: Phoenix led 102–90 with 1:44 left, then San Antonio scored the last eight, including two three-pointers by San Antonio’s Brown. Goodwin’s extra chances and Dunn’s pressure remain the spine; carrying those gains through the closing stretch is what makes them dependable. [NBA · Saturday closing sequence]

As the remaining preseason games arrive, I would follow what happens just after a substitution and just after a mistake. Does the next group continue making the opponent work? Does a lost possession turn into several, or does the team recover its attention on the next trip?

Phoenix has already shown players who can improve a possession through their effort. Carrying that contribution from one group to the next would make the team easier to trust over a full night.

Competition, continuity & reach

Official · WNBA Finals are set

Golden State and Atlanta each swept their semifinal, 3–0. Their best-of-seven Finals begin October 17. For a two-franchise owner, the longer series offers a timely view of how the league’s last two teams respond to repeated adjustments. [WNBA · 2026 playoff bracket]

Reported · Podziemski extension terms

Golden State signed Brandin Podziemski to an extension; NBA.com reported five years and $110 million. Keeping a developing contributor gives ownership another example of how a contender balances continuity with its next generation. [NBA.com · Podziemski extension]

Official · Memphis’ center depth gets tested

Isaiah Stewart will be reevaluated in two weeks after a moderate left ankle sprain. Memphis now has an early test of how its preparation holds up when availability changes. [Grizzlies · Stewart update]
