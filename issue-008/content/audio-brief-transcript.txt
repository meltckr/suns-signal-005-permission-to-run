Matt, Oso Ighodaro’s week starts with one revealing detail.

His free youth camp at Pecos Community Center began with breathing and meditation.

Roughly 50 children were in the gym where he grew up, along with trainers and coaches who helped form him. Oso called the return super special. He also said mindfulness is now an important part of his own routine.

That calm carries into the role he is describing for Phoenix.

Oso played all 82 games last season, one of 18 NBA players to do it. He started 24 and averaged 6.5 points, 5.1 rebounds, and 2.3 assists in 22 minutes while shooting 65.3 percent from the field.

When he started, the responsibility grew. He averaged 8 points, 6.3 rebounds, and 3.1 assists in 26.9 minutes. Then he started all four playoff games against Oklahoma City and averaged 7.5 points, 7 rebounds, and 4 assists in 32.3 minutes.

The useful pattern is that his connective value held as the stage got larger.

Now he is giving the role a name. He wants to quarterback the floor offensively and defensively, lead more, and play with greater aggression.

The next bet is measurable.

Oso made 53 of 117 free throws last season, 45.3 percent, after shooting 58 percent as a rookie. He attempted one three last season and is 0 for 3 in his career. The Arizona Republic reported that 87.5 percent of his shots came inside 10 feet.

The goal is not to turn him into a different player. It is to expand what the connector role can hold. A more dependable free throw and credible range could change how defenses guard his handoffs, short rolls, and late-clock decisions.

This connects to his larger point about Phoenix.

ESPN projected the Suns eighth in the West at 42 and 40, three wins below last season. Oso said expectations may look about the same from the outside. Inside, he said the goal remains to compete in the playoffs and for a championship.

That gap is not a grievance. It is a standard.

Availability built the foundation. Voice and responsibility can deepen it. The free throw and three will show whether the next layer is real.

Much love, my brother, dominate!
